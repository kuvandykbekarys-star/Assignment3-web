Popular Coffee Types

Three simple drinks that you can find in almost every coffee shop.

Espresso

A small and strong coffee with a rich taste. It is a base for many other coffee drinks.

Cappuccino

Espresso with steamed milk and milk foam. It has a softer and creamy taste.

Latte

Coffee with more milk and a mild taste. It is a popular choice for beginners.
